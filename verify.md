<div class="wrap page">
<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">Security</p><h1>Verify before you run</h1><p class="lead">A download is not trustworthy merely because the filename looks correct.</p></header>
<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><h2>Confirm the exact release</h2><p>Check version, operating system, architecture and the official release location.</p></div>
<div class="step"><h2>Check SHA-256</h2><p>Calculate the file hash locally and compare it with the exact published checksum.</p></div>
<div class="step"><h2>Check provenance</h2><p>Where release provenance or signing data is published, verify that the artifact corresponds to the intended source revision and build process.</p></div>
<div class="step"><h2>Do not bypass warnings casually</h2><p>If a checksum, provenance record or expected release identity does not match, stop and investigate before execution.</p></div>
