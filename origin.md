<h1>Who Is Building VALDR</h1>
<p class="lead">VALDR is being developed through a human-directed, AI-assisted engineering workflow.</p>
</header>

<p>The project was initiated and is directed by <strong>Alexander Shevchuk</strong>, who defines the goals, architecture priorities, product direction and final decisions.</p>

<p>Development is carried out with the assistance of <strong>ChatGPT as an AI development tool and technical partner</strong>. AI-assisted workflows are used for architecture review, code generation, technical documentation, testing strategy, analysis and implementation support.</p>

<h2>Human-directed development</h2>
<p>VALDR is not an autonomous AI project. Design choices, approvals, releases and project responsibility remain under human control.</p>

<h2>A practical engineering model</h2>
<p>Instead of depending on a large traditional team from the beginning, VALDR combines human direction, open-source development practices, automated testing and AI-assisted engineering.</p>

<p>As the project grows, additional contributors may participate in code review, testing, infrastructure, documentation and network operation. The technical history of the project remains visible through source control, specifications and reproducible development stages.</p>

<h2>Transparent by construction</h2>
<p>VALDR is being built through a transparent engineering process in which a human project owner works with modern AI-assisted development tools and open-source infrastructure.</p>
